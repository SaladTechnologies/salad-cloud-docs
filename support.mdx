## Contact Support

SaladCloud Support is available Monday through Friday, 9am to 5pm Pacific Time. Requests are handled in the order they
are received.

- **Email** [cloud@salad.com](mailto:cloud@salad.com) from the email address on your SaladCloud account.
- **Use the support widget** in the bottom right corner of this site, or the help icon in the top navigation bar of the
  [SaladCloud Portal](https://portal.salad.com).

See [Contact Support](/support/contact) for details on the support widgets.

### What to Include

Include these details so we can help you without asking follow-up questions:

- Your organization and project names.
- The product you are using, and the container group name, job ID, or request ID if you have one.
- The instance or machine ID, if the problem affects specific instances.
- When the problem happened, with the date, time, and time zone.
- The exact error message, status code, or log output.
- What you expected to happen, and what you have already tried.

<Warning>Never send API keys, passwords, or other secrets in a support request.</Warning>
